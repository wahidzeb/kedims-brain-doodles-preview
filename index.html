About Kedim's Brain Doodles

Kedim's Brain Doodles is a free brain teaser game with Kedim, the orange cat. Every level is one tricky doodle picture set in a Malaysian place: a mamak stall, a kampung, the beach at night, a kitchen or a Hari Raya evening. Read the question, look closely and think a little sideways.

There are 40 puzzles in 4 packs, and each pack teaches a new trick: tap the hidden answer, drag things around the picture, draw the missing part with your finger, and trace a whole picture in one line without lifting. Each puzzle takes under a minute, gives up to 3 stars, and the read-aloud hint is always free.

3 quick tips

  1. Read every word of the question. The trick is often hiding in the words!
  2. Stuck? Tap the light bulb for a free hint, and tap it again to see where to look.
  3. In One Line puzzles, start on a dot that has an odd number of lines.
